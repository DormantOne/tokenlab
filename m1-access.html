Getting in the door

Before you can study a model, you need one to talk to. There are good free doors, two of them through Pitt, and paid tiers if you want more room. This module helps you pick, get at least one account working, and learn the rule that matters more than which model you use.

Your instructor

Free compute is available inside the system, so try the Pitt route first. The free tiers of ChatGPT and Claude are reasonable, Gemini is free, and Arena lets you use advanced models for free. For about $20 a month, OpenAI or Anthropic gets you to the frontier, or close to it. Easy on, easy cancel.

Claude adds

Agreed. One addition: for coursework and anything sensitive, what an account does with your data matters more than which model it runs.

Through Pitt, free

Claude, through Pitt

Free with your Pitt account

Anthropic's Claude, with Pitt's agreement around it. It includes Projects, which you'll use in Module 7.

  1. Go to Microsoft My Apps and sign in with your Pitt account.
  2. Choose Add apps, then Request new apps, and search for "Claude". If it asks why, one sentence is enough, such as "I need Claude for my class."
  3. Add it. In about an hour it appears under Pitt Digital Software Distribution Services. Access inside Claude itself can take a little longer to catch up.
  4. Go to claude.ai and sign in through Pitt Passport. Your name should appear at the bottom left, with Pitt as the organization.

Your data: Pitt says Claude won't use Pitt data to train its models. Before uploading anything beyond public or fictional material, check Pitt's approved tools and data classifications.

Source: Pitt Digital, Claude for Education

Microsoft Copilot Chat, through Pitt

Free for Pitt students, faculty and staff

Microsoft's assistant, signed in with your Pitt account. Microsoft Copilot is also the AI tool UPMC approves for its staff.

Open Copilot Chat and sign in with your Pitt account. Pitt's list of AI tools also includes PittGPT.

Source: Pitt AI tools overview

On your own, free

ChatGPT

Free. Go is $8 a month, Plus $20.

The lab's teach-backs use ChatGPT, so you'll want at least the free account. Sign up at chatgpt.com.

Your data: OpenAI may use your conversations to improve its models unless you opt out. The switch is in Settings, under Data controls, called "Improve the model for everyone." OpenAI has also said it will test ads in the Free and Go tiers in the US, while Plus and higher stay ad-free.

Sources: OpenAI, What is ChatGPT Plus?; OpenAI, Introducing ChatGPT Go

Claude, personal

Free. Pro is $20 a month in the US.

The same models as Pitt's Claude, on a personal account at claude.ai. If you have Pitt access, use that for coursework instead.

Your data: Personal Claude accounts (Free, Pro and Max) have a model-training setting under Settings, then Privacy. Turn it off, and new chats aren't used for future training.

Sources: Claude Help Center, What is the Pro plan?; Anthropic Privacy Center

Gemini

Free with a Google account

Google's assistant is at gemini.google.com. For experiments, Google AI Studio lets you try Google's models in the browser.

Your data: Google's pricing page marks free-tier AI Studio and API use as used to improve its products. Google has also been narrowing which models free users get, so check what you're offered.

Source: Gemini API pricing

Arena

Free

Ask one question and get answers from two anonymous models side by side. Vote, then see which was which. A blind comparison is a real experimental tool, and you'll use it in Module 3. Start at Arena's side-by-side page.

Your data: Treat Arena as public. Its terms say conversations may be shared with the model providers and published to support research, and it asks you not to submit anything personal or sensitive.

Source: Arena FAQ

Paying for more

For about $20 a month, ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro gets you stronger models and much more room to work. Both bill month to month. ChatGPT Plus cancels from Settings, then Account. ChatGPT Go, at $8, sits in between: more use of the everyday model, without most of Plus's advanced tools.

Claude adds

A paid personal plan is still a personal account. Paying buys usage and models; it doesn't change whether your chats can be used for training. That's still the setting described above.

Prices and settings were checked on November 21, 2026, and they change often. The source links above have the current details.

The rules for this lab

  1. Fictional cases only. Never paste patient information into any tool in this lab, Pitt's included. Every case in this lab is made up, and anything you add should be too.
  2. Personal accounts get public or fictional material only. Check the data setting on each one you use.
  3. Arena is public. Write nothing there you wouldn't post online.
  4. UPMC devices and networks redirect unapproved AI sites to Copilot, so the ChatGPT steps won't work there. Do the teach-backs from another device and network, or ask your instructor to switch the lab to Copilot.

Source for the UPMC redirect: Pitt Digital, Claude for Education

Your checklist

First contact (optional)

Ask two different models the same question, and note one difference. A starting question:

In three sentences, explain what you are. Then name one thing you're unsure about regarding yourself.

Your first teach-back

This is the loop you'll use in every module, so it's worth doing once on an easy topic. Write your answer, copy the prompt into ChatGPT, then paste ChatGPT's reply back here. The lab checks the reply's format and saves it in your notebook.
